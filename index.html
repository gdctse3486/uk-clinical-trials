Source & methodology. Data are retrieved live from the U.S. National Library of Medicine's ClinicalTrials.gov API v2. A study is included when its record contains a UK location (country = United Kingdom); the lead sponsor is used for sponsor attribution, not collaborators. “Ongoing / not yet recruiting” includes Recruiting, Not yet recruiting, Active, not recruiting, Enrolling by invitation and Suspended. Phase may be missing or not applicable for observational studies. Therapy areas are heuristic groupings inferred from condition terms and are not official registry classifications. Study-drug charts use DRUG and BIOLOGICAL intervention names supplied by each record; some trials may have multiple interventions or no named drug. City and site counts reflect listed location entries, not unique hospitals or patient numbers. The API is updated as registry records change; figures can differ from other registries and do not represent every UK trial. Company wordmarks/colours are illustrative brand treatments, not official logos or endorsements.
